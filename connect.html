Connecting AMP Hub to QuickBooks Online

AMP Hub is AMP's internal application and runs on AMP's own office server. It is not available to the public. To connect or reconnect it to AMP's QuickBooks Online company:

  1. Sign in to AMP Hub on the office network and open the Projects page.
  2. Click Connect to QuickBooks and sign in to Intuit as the QuickBooks company administrator.
  3. Choose the AMP company and allow access. You are returned to AMP Hub and the Projects page shows the connection as active.

The connection renews itself. If it has lapsed after a long period without use, repeat the steps above.
