AMP Hub Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

AMP Hub is an internal business application operated by AMP ("we", "us") for its own employees. It is not offered to the public and is not available to anyone outside the company.

What the application does

AMP Hub connects to AMP's own QuickBooks Online company and AMP's own BambooHR account, using Intuit's and BambooHR's official APIs, to prepare weekly payroll job costing, project reports and related internal reports.

Information we access

  • From QuickBooks Online: customers and projects, accounts, classes, transactions (bills, purchases, invoices, journal entries), budgets and financial reports of AMP's own company file.
  • From BambooHR: AMP employee names, employee numbers, departments, timesheet entries and pay rates.

All of this information belongs to AMP and concerns AMP's own business and employees.

How the information is used

The information is used only to run AMP's internal payroll job costing and project reporting. It is not used for marketing, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party other than the service providers named above, which we already use to run the business.

Where the information is stored

AMP Hub runs on a server owned and controlled by AMP inside its office network. Data pulled from QuickBooks Online and BambooHR is stored on that server only. Access to the application requires signing in, and the QuickBooks connection can be revoked at any time from within the application or from the QuickBooks Online "Apps" settings.

Retention

Weekly payroll data and generated reports are kept as long as AMP needs them for accounting and record-keeping purposes and are covered by AMP's normal backups.

Security

API credentials are stored on the server and are never sent to users' browsers. The application is reachable only from AMP's internal network.
